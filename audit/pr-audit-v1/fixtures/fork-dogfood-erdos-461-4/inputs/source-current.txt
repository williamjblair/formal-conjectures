Let s_t(n) be the t-smooth component of n: the product, with multiplicity, of all primes p dividing n such that p < t. Let f(n,t) count the number of distinct values of s_t(m) for m in [n+1,n+t]. Is f(n,t) bounded below by a positive constant times t, uniformly for all t and n?

The page notes the known weaker lower bound of order t / log t.
